Who I Am

Oakland Bay Area born and raised and now based in Nashville, Tennessee, I've kept music, guitar, and artists at the center of my life since I was a kid. My mission is simple: replenish artists and music organizations with new possibilities in the studio and beyond.

From playing guitar in ska-punk and rock bands to engineering on large-format consoles and analog tape in the biggest studios in the world, I've been lucky to make music with the best: Grateful Dead, Bob Weir, Lukas Nelson, Tyler Childers, Joe Bonamassa, G-Eazy, Alphabet Rockers (GRAMMY winners), Slightly Stoopid, The National, Journey, Joe Satriani, and many more.

I'm an active strategist and coach to creators worldwide and co-developed the nonprofit music accelerator ZOOLABS, helping hundreds of artists and startups align their art, business, and purpose. In 2021, I helped build the artist experience at Songfinch, connecting thousands of artists and fans.

Whether we are cutting live tracks to tape, dialing in iconic electric and acoustic guitar tones, mixing your next record, or mapping out your career strategy, I'm dedicated to making the music industry more creative and sustainable.
